## 🏗️ The Solution

### What We Built
An **AI-powered early warning system** that works like a tireless research team, operating 24/7 to:

1. **🔍 Scan the World** - Every night at midnight, AI agents search:
   - PubMed (medical research)
   - ClinicalTrials.gov (trial data)
   - FDA databases (approvals, safety alerts)

2. **🤖 Analyze with AI** - Claude 3.5 (Amazon's advanced AI) reads every document and:
   - Identifies competitive threats
   - Extracts key insights
   - Assesses business impact
   - Spots opportunities

3. **📧 Deliver Intelligence** - Every morning at 9 AM, you get:
   - Personalized digest for your watchlist
   - Only critical insights, no noise
   - Actionable recommendations
   - Source citations for verification

4. **💬 Interactive Chat** - Ask questions anytime:
   - "What's the latest on Humira biosimilars?"
   - "Show me all CAR-T therapy trials"
   - "What are the risks for our lung cancer program?"

### How It Works (Simple Version)

```
Night (Midnight):
```
