# USA Healthcare Competitive Intelligence Domain Guide

## 🏥 Market Overview

### Market Size & Growth
- **Total Healthcare Market**: $4.3 trillion (2021)
- **Pharmaceutical Market**: $550+ billion annually
- **Biotech Market**: $175+ billion
- **Medical Device Market**: $208+ billion
- **Annual Growth Rate**: 5-7% across sectors

### Key Market Segments
- **Prescription Drugs**: 85% of pharma revenue
- **Biologics**: Fastest growing segment (12% CAGR)
- **Generics**: 90% of prescriptions, 20% of revenue
- **Specialty Drugs**: 50% of spending, <5% of volume

## 🏛️ Regulatory Landscape

### FDA (Food and Drug Administration)
- **Drug Approval Process**: IND → Phase I/II/III → NDA/BLA
- **Average Timeline**: 10-15 years, $2.6 billion cost
- **Breakthrough Therapy**: Expedited review (6 months)
- **Fast Track**: For unmet medical needs
- **Priority Review**: 6-month vs 10-month standard

### Key Regulatory Pathways
- **505(b)(1)**: New molecular entities
- **505(b)(2)**: Modified existing drugs
- **ANDA**: Generic drug applications
- **Biosimilar Pathway**: Follow-on biologics

### Critical Regulatory Events
- **PDUFA Dates**: FDA action deadlines
- **AdComm Meetings**: Advisory committee reviews
- **Complete Response Letters**: FDA feedback/rejections
- **Label Negotiations**: Post-approval discussions

## 💊 Pharmaceutical Intelligence Focus Areas

### Drug Development Pipeline
- **Preclinical Research**: Target identification, lead optimization
- **Clinical Trials**: Phase I (safety) → Phase II (efficacy) → Phase III (confirmatory)
- **Regulatory Submission**: NDA/BLA filing and review
- **Commercial Launch**: Market access, pricing, competition

### Competitive Monitoring Targets
- **First-in-Class**: Novel mechanisms of action
- **Best-in-Class**: Improved efficacy/safety profiles
- **Biosimilars**: Generic biologics competition
- **Combination Therapies**: Multi-drug approaches
- **Orphan Drugs**: Rare disease treatments

### Key Intelligence Sources
- **ClinicalTrials.gov**: 400,000+ registered studies
- **PubMed**: 34+ million biomedical citations
- **FDA Orange Book**: Approved drug products
- **FDA Purple Book**: Licensed biological products
- **SEC Filings**: Financial and pipeline disclosures
